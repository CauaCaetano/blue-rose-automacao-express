    Before you reach out.

    Will the automation message my customers on its own?

    Only within rules you approve, like replying to someone who messaged or reminding a pending payment. AI agents that write proposals always stop for your approval.

    Do I need a new WhatsApp number?

    No. The automation works with the number your business already uses, with no new line and nothing that already works being touched.

    Do I need to be tech-savvy?

    No. Once it is ready, if you want to change a price, your hours or a service, just message me. You never have to touch code or a dashboard.

    Is the website in my name?

    Yes, the content and structure are yours. If you want your own domain (yourbusiness.com) instead of the default link, we can arrange it separately.

    Can I ask for changes after it is done?

    One round of tweaks is included right after delivery. Bigger changes after that are quoted separately, always agreed before I touch anything.
